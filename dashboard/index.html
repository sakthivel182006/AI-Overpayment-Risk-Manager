Governance Notice

Risk scores are review signals, not findings of improper payment. Population-level disparities should be investigated before operational deployment.

Methodology

How the AI Risk Manager generates investigator signals

01

Payment Analysis

Payment history is analyzed against the expected monthly award to identify unusually large payments.

02

Pattern Detection

Payment frequency, variability, adjustments and repeated high-payment months contribute to the risk signal.

03

Explainable Scoring

Multiple anomaly signals are combined into a 0–100 explainable risk score.

04

Human Review

Only the highest-ranked cases are surfaced to investigators. The system does not automatically determine improper payment.

👤

Human-in-the-loop

Every flagged case requires investigator review. Administrative activity may have legitimate explanations and should not automatically increase risk.
